# Existenz Community Engine

A community link-aggregation and discussion platform organized around a curated Main Feed, independent Subfeeds, and personal User Feeds, driven by community standing, organic traction, and editorial governance.

## Feeds & Origins

**Main Feed**:
The primary front-page stream combining native editorial submissions and promoted content from Subfeeds and User Feeds.
_Avoid_: Front page, global feed, home stream

**Subfeed**:
A topic-scoped community desk with its own member roster, local governance roles (owner, moderator, editor, uploader), and discounted promotion threshold into the Main Feed.
_Avoid_: Subreddit, channel, group, category

**User Feed**:
A member's personal publishing stream where newcomers and members publish without needing Subfeed membership or Main Feed standing.
_Avoid_: Profile wall, personal blog, user channel

## Visibility, Promotion & Ranking

**Content Visibility**:
The rule set determining whether a Link or Post may be seen on a read surface, accounting for publication state, soft-deletion/removal, quarantine, and viewer NSFW preference.
_Avoid_: Safety filter, read filter, access check

**Promotion Gate**:
The unified policy deciding whether non-Main content (from a Subfeed or User Feed) qualifies to appear in the Main Feed via organic thresholds, featured status, or curated Discovery Boost.
_Avoid_: Trending filter, viral threshold, main feed filter

**Discovery Boost**:
A curated promotion pathway allowing members with sufficient decayed discovery standing (or community moderators) to surface safe Subfeed or User Feed content into the Main Feed.
_Avoid_: Manual promote, pin, upvote boost

**Editorial Priority**:
A time-bounded (24-hour) ranking boost granted when an editor or moderator publishes directly to the Main Feed, expiring automatically once the window elapses.
_Avoid_: Pinned post, sticky boost, staff pick

**Derived Signals**:
The computed engagement, velocity, controversy, spam/ragebait probabilities, and ranking score maintained on a Link or Post from its votes, comments, clicks, and discoveries.
_Avoid_: Metadata, stats, counters

## Standing

**Trust Progression**:
A member's position on the trust ladder, expressed as the level they hold, the next level available, how far along the band they are, and how much effective standing remains.
_Avoid_: XP bar, level bar, progress widget
